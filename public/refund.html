2. Refunds and remedies the law requires

Because all purchases are final, we provide a refund or another remedy only where the law requires it. Examples include:

  • Paid but no code delivered. If payment cleared and no code was generated, or the success page could not show it, we will deliver the code you paid for. If we cannot deliver it, we will refund the charge.
  • Duplicate charge. If you were charged more than once for the same purchase — for example two successful payments for one pack, or two leaderboard entries for the same week — we will refund the duplicate charge.
  • Unauthorised payment. If a payment was made without your authorisation, report it to us and to your bank or card issuer. Once we verify it, we will refund the amount to the original payment method.
  • Wrong amount. If you were charged an amount that does not match the price shown at checkout, we will refund the difference or the full charge.
  • Payment reversed by the processor. If our payment processor confirms that a payment failed or was reversed but an item was still issued, we will cancel the item or refund the charge so you are not charged for something you did not pay for.
Where the law requires a refund, redemption does not remove it. If a duplicate or unauthorised payment produced a code that was already redeemed, the law still requires the charge to be refunded and the item reversed as described in section 5.

3. No voluntary refund window

We do not offer a discretionary refund window. A purchase is not refundable simply because you changed your mind, bought the wrong item, or did not use it. Any refund or remedy we provide is the one the law requires; statutory time limits apply.

This is not the prize claim window. Prizes from the weekly leaderboard are governed by the Terms of Service, including eligibility, verification, the prize claim window and the payout timeline. This policy applies only to purchases of codes.

4. Purchases we do not refund

Because all purchases are final and non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise, we do not refund:

  • A delivered code, whether or not you have redeemed it, and whether or not you still have the code. Lost codes can be recovered free of charge (section 6).
  • Anime Coins that have already been used — for example on hints, timer pauses, stakes, gauntlet retries or other in-game spending.
  • An activated Premium Pass, in whole or in part, whether or not you used its benefits.
  • An activated Weekly Leaderboard Entry once it has been applied to that week's prize pool, or once the week's competition has closed.
  • Buying the wrong pack or the wrong quantity, or changing your mind after paying.
  • Not using the app, or losing access to your account, including forgetting your password.
  • Suspension or termination for breach of our Terms, or items removed under our fair-play and anti-cheat rules.

If your code was never delivered, that is a section 2 case and we will deliver or refund it — do not assume it is covered by this section.

5. What happens to the item after a refund

Where the law requires a refund, it reverses both the money and the item. Depending on what was delivered and used, we will take the following steps:

  • Unused code: we cancel the code so it can no longer be redeemed.
  • AC already credited: we deduct the equivalent AC from your balance. If some of those coins were already spent, we deduct what remains and may restrict the account until the shortfall is settled or offset.
  • Premium Pass: we end the pass that was refunded, so only a pass you actually paid for remains active.
  • Weekly Leaderboard Entry: before the week's snapshot is taken, we remove the entry and correct the pool. If the week has already been snapshotted and paid out, the entry cannot be withdrawn; we will still refund the duplicate or unauthorised charge and absorb the pooled amount.

We do this so a refund cannot be used to keep both the money and the item.

6. Failed delivery and code recovery

  • After payment, your code is shown on the success page. You can return to that page at any time and retrieve the code with your transaction reference and the email you used at checkout.
  • Recovery is free and unlimited. Losing a code is not a refund reason because you can always retrieve it.
  • If you cannot retrieve a code for a payment that cleared, email animequizzm@gmail.com with your transaction reference, purchase email and the date. We will trace the payment and deliver the code, or refund it if delivery is not possible.

7. How to request a refund or remedy

  1. Email animequizzm@gmail.com with: the email you used at checkout, your transaction reference (shown on the success or receipt page), the date of purchase, what went wrong, and the legal right or remedy you are asking us to apply.
  2. We may ask for information needed to verify the payment or the account, such as a bank or card statement showing the charge. Never send your full card number, CVV or bank password.
  3. We aim to decide on your request and submit any refund the law requires within 3 business days of receiving the information we need. If we need more time, we will tell you why and give a new date.
  4. If a refund is required, we submit it to the original payment method.

8. How refunds are paid

  • Where a refund is required, it is made through Flutterwave, our payment processor, back to the payment method used for the original charge. We do not refund to a different card, bank account or wallet.
  • Refund timing depends on the payment method: card refunds usually complete within 3–15 days, bank transfers within about 24 hours, and mobile money within 3–5 days. Your bank may add its own processing time.
  • Refunds are in the same currency as the original charge (Naira). We do not control exchange-rate differences applied by your bank.
  • If only part of a charge was wrong, we will refund the difference rather than the full amount.

These timings are set by the payment method, your bank and the payment provider. They control when the refund actually arrives in your account; we do not.

9. Unauthorised payments and chargebacks

If you see an AnimeQuiz charge you do not recognise, contact us first at animequizzm@gmail.com so we can investigate quickly. You can also report it to your bank or card issuer. We cooperate with payment-processor and bank investigations, and we may pause the account connected to the payment while a dispute is open so no further purchases or redemptions happen on it. If a chargeback is later reversed in our favour, we may restore the item or the account.

10. Prizes and in-game stakes are not purchases

  • Cash prizes won in the Weekly Leaderboard Competition are governed by the Terms of Service, including eligibility, verification, the prize claim window and the payout timeline. Prize payouts are not refunds.
  • In-game stakes you place with other players (AC or WP) are returned to your in-game balance when a challenge expires or is voided, less the applicable stake fee. These are in-game credit movements, not refunds of money you paid us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or on the website before they take effect. The version in effect when you made your purchase applies to that purchase.

    Installing

    1. Download the file

      Tap Download APK above. Android will save it to your Downloads folder.

    2. Allow installs from your browser

      Android blocks app installs by default. When you tap the downloaded file, choose Settings → Allow from this source, then go back and confirm the install.

    3. Tap the downloaded file to install

      Confirm with Install. It replaces your existing copy — you will not lose your coins or account.

    4. Open AnimeQuiz

      Sign in as normal. The update checks itself in the background.

    Good to know
    Old versions eventually stop working. When we ship an important fix we raise the minimum build, and an outdated install is blocked from playing until it updates. The app tells you when this happens.
    Only download from this page. We are not on the Play Store. An APK from anywhere else may be modified, and we cannot support it.
    Something not working? If the install fails, or your phone is on an Android version we no longer support, email us at animequizzm@gmail.com with your phone model and Android version.

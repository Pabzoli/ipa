Yes. Installing and playing is free. Coins, Premium and leaderboard entries are optional paid extras.

Your code appears on the success page the moment payment clears, and you can recover it there any time by signing in.

Why verify my email?

Verification protects your account and is required for purchases, leaderboard prizes and payouts. It takes under a minute from the app.

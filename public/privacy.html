Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Effective: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anime Quiz ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares, stores and deletes personal data when you use the AnimeQuiz mobile app and website (together, the "Service"). We are the data controller for the processing described here. You can contact us at animequizzm@gmail.com.

1. Who this policy applies to

  • Anyone who creates an AnimeQuiz account or plays the quiz.
  • Buyers who purchase Anime Coins, a Premium Pass (28 days), or a Weekly Leaderboard Entry on our website.
  • Winners who submit bank details to receive a cash prize.
  • Anyone who contacts support, submits feedback, or asks us to recover a purchase or process a refund.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and let you play.
  • To operate weekly leaderboards and calculate prize eligibility.
  • To detect cheating, collusion, multi-accounting and other abuse.
  • To deliver codes, recover codes for buyers, and investigate payments that were duplicated, failed or not authorised.
  • To process refunds requested within the refund request window (3 days from code delivery), and to investigate failed delivery, duplicate charges, unauthorised payments, wrong amounts and processor reversals.
  • To process prize payouts, and to keep the records the law requires.
  • To show ads (unless you are a Premium subscriber).
  • To answer support requests and comply with law and our Terms of Service.

5. Data retention

  • Account and gameplay data: permanently deleted when you delete your account (see section 7), including your profile, your email address and its uniqueness record, match history, quiz sessions, gauntlet entries, coin/point transaction logs, seen-question markers, and unused/used redemption-code receipts held under your account.
  • Fraud and security signals (IP, timing): deleted on account deletion, or retained only for the period needed for an anti-fraud investigation, whichever is later.
  • Bank account details: purged immediately after a prize is paid. On completion of a transfer we delete the account number, bank code/name and beneficiary name and keep only a masked reference (for example ****1234) plus the payment reference. They are also deleted on account deletion unless a payout is still in progress.
  • Shared records you appear in (anonymised, not deleted): your username shown in other players' match history, in weekly results, and in the ghost/opponent pool is replaced with a neutral label on deletion; the surrounding record is kept so other players' histories and our audit trail stay intact.
  • Financial records (retained by law): completed purchase records, refund records, payout and transfer ledgers, audit entries, house-fee accounting, and redeemed-code records are retained for 7 years for accounting, tax, anti-fraud and dispute resolution. Personal identifiers in these records are removed on account deletion where they are not needed for that legal purpose.
  • Support and feedback messages: kept while your request is open and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can handle follow-ups and disputes.

6. How we protect your data

  • Access to economy and balance fields is server-authoritative; clients cannot alter coin or point balances.
  • Payment, refund and payout flows require server-side verification, including payment-processor webhook signature checks.
  • Least-privilege access controls. Secrets are held server-side and are never shipped in the client.
  • Transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit.

No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and account/data deletion

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local authority.
How to delete your account: In the app, go to Profile → Account → Danger zone → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately. You can also email animequizzm@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my account"; we action verified email requests within 7 days.

What deletion does: it permanently removes your Firebase sign-in account, your profile and profile sub-data (match history, quiz sessions, gauntlet entries, coin/point logs, seen-question markers, receipts, and bank/payout details), your matchmaking-queue entry, your anti-fraud signals, and any pending prize claim. Your name is anonymised in records shared with other players. Completed financial records (purchases, refunds, payout ledger, house-fee accounting, redeemed codes) are retained as required by law, with personal details removed where not legally needed. If you have an outstanding prize payout or an open refund or payment dispute, deletion may be blocked until it is settled so your money is not lost.

8. Children

The Service is for users aged 18 and over because it involves cash prizes. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and the website

The shop and payment pages use only what is necessary to work and to process payment through our processor. We do not currently run analytics or advertising cookies or tags on the website. If that changes, we will update this policy and add a consent mechanism where the law requires one. Payment is completed on Flutterwave's hosted checkout, which has its own privacy and cookie terms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email before they take effect.
